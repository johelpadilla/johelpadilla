# Johel Padilla-Villanueva, DrPH

I am an Adjunct Professor in the Department of Environmental Health, Graduate School of Public Health, University of Puerto Rico, Medical Sciences Campus. I develop ordinal methods for multivariate time series, chiefly Systemic Tau (τₛ) and the Discrete Extramental Clock (RECD), and study their use as early-warning signals of critical transitions in complex systems, including epidemiological and clinical data.

## Research focus

- **Systemic Tau (τₛ):** ordinal concordance as a measure of how the parts of a multivariate system reorganize their mutual relations.
- **Discrete Extramental Clock (RECD):** a discrete time generator built from ordinal concordance, with nested conjunction levels Φ₁–Φ₃ and the excess³ proxy for order-3 synergy.
- **Early-warning signals in health data:** dengue surveillance series (San Juan and Iquitos) and heart-rate dynamics before spontaneous ventricular fibrillation (PhysioNet SDDB).
- **Reproducibility:** frozen analysis protocols, false-alarm controls, and machine-checkable formalization in Lean 4.
